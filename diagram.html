4Operations Concepts Applied

This is where your OPS class becomes a competitive edge

Operations Concept Application to CareFlow
🔴 Bottlenecks Identify whether beds, staffing, transfers, or discharge delays are the binding constraint on patient flow
📊 Capacity Utilization Understand which facilities are overloaded and which have genuinely usable capacity — not just open beds
📐 Queueing Theory Estimate how variable patient arrival rates and treatment times interact to create unpredictable waits
🔁 Process Flow Map each step from arrival → evaluation → admission → transfer → discharge to find time waste
📏 Little's Law Relate arrival rate, average wait time, and number of patients in the system quantitatively

5Little's Law — The Math Behind the Wait

This formula gives CareFlow its quantitative backbone

The fundamental relationship between waiting and throughput
L = λ × W
L
Avg # patients
in the system
λ
Arrival rate
(patients/hour)
W
Avg time

If you know how many patients are boarding (L) and the arrival rate (λ), you can infer the average boarding duration (W) — and identify where interventions have the biggest impact.
